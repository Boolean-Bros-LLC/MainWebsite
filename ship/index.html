[SECTION 04]

The rejections that actually happen.

  • 4.2 — minimum functionality. "Primarily a repackaged website." Fixed with native capability, not an argument.
  • 5.1.1(v) — account deletion. If the app creates accounts, it has to let people delete them, server-side.
  • 5.1.2 — tracking. App Tracking Transparency, declared honestly, or no tracking SDK at all.
  • 3.1.1 — payments. Digital goods go through in-app purchase. There is no clever third option.
  • ITMS-91053 — privacy manifest. A required-reason API your build uses that PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy does not declare.
  • ITMS-90717 — icon alpha. Transparency in a 1024px icon. It fails at upload, silently, until you know.

Questions worth asking.

The build and submission work is usually two to four days. Review adds whatever the store decides: a day, sometimes a week. Android is faster than iOS almost every time.

I fix what they named and resubmit. That is included through first acceptance, not billed as a new round. If the rejection needs real product work rather than a fix, I will tell you before doing it.
